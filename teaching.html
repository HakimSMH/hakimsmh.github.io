Teaching

I teach in the EEA department (Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Automatic Control) of the Faculty of Science and Technology (FST) at the University of Lille: around 200 hours a year, from undergraduate to Master's level, on interaction, immersive technologies and embedded systems. I am also involved in building neurotechnology curricula, in Lille and at the European level within NeurotechEU.
